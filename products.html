Handmade Daily in Small Batches

All items are baked fresh every morning using stone-ground flours, pure dairy butter, and time-tested recipes. Because we bake in limited batches, we recommend visiting early or placing an inquiry through our pre-order request form.

Artisan Hearth Breads

Priced between 6.50 USD and 10.50 USD per loaf

  • Heritage Country Sourdough: 80% extraction wheat, whole rye, and sea salt with a blistered crust.
  • Seeded Multigrain: Packed with toasted sunflower, sesame, pumpkin, and flax seeds.
  • Rustic French Baguette: Crisp exterior with an open, custardy honeycomb crumb.
  • Cranberry Walnut Batard: Tangy sourdough folded with toasted walnuts and tart dried cranberries.

Viennoiserie & Morning Pastries

Priced between 3.75 USD and 5.50 USD per piece

  • Classic Butter Croissant: Layered with fermented European-style butter for sixty-four distinct flaky sheets.
  • Pain au Chocolat: Folded with two batons of bittersweet 60% dark chocolate.
  • Seasonal Fruit Danish: Filled with vanilla bean pastry cream and stone fruits or berries from local orchards.
  • Almond Frangipane Croissant: Twice-baked, brushed with orange blossom syrup, and topped with sliced almonds.

Layer Cakes & Gathering Sweets

Priced between 32.00 USD and 58.00 USD (available primarily by advance request)

  • Carrot & Toasted Pecan Cake: Spiced carrot sponge layered with whipped mascarpone cream cheese frosting.
  • Dark Chocolate Olive Oil Cake: Moist, rich single-tier cake dusted with cocoa and Maldon sea salt flakes.
  • Lemon Buttermilk Bundt: Tart, tender crumb soaked in fresh lemon verbena glaze.
