Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2017 inside a small garage with a single converted pizza oven and a passion for wild fermentation. Founders Clara Mendez and David Ross believed that bread shouldn't just be a quick vehicle for a sandwich—it should be a nutritious staple made from simple ingredients that connects us with regional agriculture.

After three years of selling at Saturday morning farmers' markets, we opened our permanent storefront on North Star Way. Today, our neighborhood shop serves as an everyday meeting spot for friends, families, and early-morning workers.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts long before flour arrives on our workbenches. We believe in transparency at every step of the food chain:

  • Organic Grains: 100% of our flours are certified organic and unbleached, free of potassium bromate or artificial improvers.
  • Regional Farming Partners: We purchase rye, spelt, and hard red winter wheat directly from family farms within a three-hundred-mile radius.
  • Pasture-Raised Dairy: Our whole milk, buttermilk, and unsalted European-style butter come from grass-fed dairy cooperatives.
  • No Synthetic Preservatives: We rely exclusively on time, salt, and natural acidity to protect and preserve our bread naturally.
