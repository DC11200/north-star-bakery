Fresh From the Oven to Our Neighborhood

Welcome to North Star Bakery! Every morning before the sun comes up, our bakers are already mixing, kneading, and shaping batches of sourdough, flaky croissants, and morning rolls. Whether you are grabbing a quick coffee on your morning commute or ordering an assortment of cakes for a weekend celebration, we are glad to have a place at your table.

Featured Item of the Week: Cardamom Morning Buns

Slow-proofed laminated brioche rolled with freshly ground green cardamom, cinnamon, and raw demerara sugar for a crisp, caramelized finish.

What We're Known For

Slow Fermentation

Our sourdough loaves ferment naturally for thirty-six hours, developing a crisp crust, an airy crumb, and an easy-to-digest flavor profile.

Locally Milled Grains

We work directly with regional organic grain farmers, milling several heirloom wheats in-house each week for maximum freshness.

Celebration & Event Orders

From birthday dessert tables to boxed pastry spreads for morning work meetings, our kitchen accepts advance pre-orders throughout the week.
